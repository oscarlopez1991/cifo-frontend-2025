Technical Indicators

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

Momentum oscillator measuring speed and change of price movements. Values above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold.

MACD

Moving Average Convergence Divergence: trend-following momentum indicator showing relationship between two moving averages.

Moving Averages

Shows the average price over a period. Common types: Simple (SMA), Exponential (EMA). Used to identify trends and support/resistance.
