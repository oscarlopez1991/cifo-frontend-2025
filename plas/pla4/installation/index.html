Overview CDN

Of the acronym content delivery network, is a group of servers spread across different geographical locations worldwide to enable the quick delivery of a website’s content. It is also known as a content distribution network.

CDN advantages

  1. Faster load times globally: Closer servers to the user, reducing latency.
  2. Browser caching: Optimization next times of visiting websites.
  3. High availability: built-in redundancy and uptime.
  4. Reduced server load: Your own hosting or server doesn’t have to handle those static files.
  5. Easy to use: No need to configure anything.

CDN disadvantages

  1. Dependency on third-party availability: Strong dependency on CDN performance.
  2. Privacy concerns: Potential exposition connecting to third-party servers.
  3. No offline development: You cannot work offline.
  4. Less control over updates: There may be incompatibilities through upgrades.
